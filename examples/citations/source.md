# Example evidence convention

Version 1, 2026-10-28. Author: scientific-agents example project.
Synthetic teaching material, not an external publication.

## Identity

The example names a particular edition of its source. A later edition does not
silently replace that identity in a historical citation.

## Acceptance

Recording an artifact's identity establishes which artifact was considered.
It does not establish that a reviewer or domain owner accepted its conclusions.

\section{Question}
How does a reader recover the exact source supporting a statement?
This example uses one local teaching source, not research evidence.

\section{Source and claim}
Artifact identity does not establish acceptance of conclusions
\cite[sec.~Acceptance]{work:example-evidence-v1}.
Read that named section in \texttt{source.md} to check the statement.
The citation links to the shared bibliography entry, whose backreference returns
to this report's citing page. The section locator identifies the passage in the
source; the backreference serves a different direction of navigation.

\section{Limit}
Resolving a citation key and building a bibliography do not establish that the
source is authoritative or that the claim is scientifically correct.

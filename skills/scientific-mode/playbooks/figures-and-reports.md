# Figures and reports

Designing or revising figures, a plotbook or a report, including a feedback
round.

1. Name the reader, the tasks the pages must support and the reference
   artifacts to match. Say which visible feature answers the question.
2. Start from the equation and plan the pages: coverage, grouping and what is
   overlaid ([scientific-plotting](../../scientific-plotting/SKILL.md)).
3. Iterate at figure scale and page scale. When two layouts compete, render
   both and decide from the pages
   ([prototype, don't ask](../../principle-prototype-dont-ask/SKILL.md)).
4. Settle the plotbook so it reads on its own, then write the report around it
   ([report-writing](../../report-writing/SKILL.md)): the argument, pinned
   sources and the shared bibliography.
5. Build. Run the source check and the render gate
   ([render-review](../../render-review/SKILL.md)); fix blocking findings at
   their source.
6. Review in separate tracks: reading tasks on selected pages, copy on the
   complete draft, and numbers by a [council](../../council/SKILL.md) when
   the conclusions depend on them. Keep findings apart from risks.
7. On feedback, find the cause in the source, fix every page with that cause,
   and recheck only the affected scope.
8. Deliver the matched plotbook and report to the user's review inbox, if the
   project has one, and say what awaits their review.

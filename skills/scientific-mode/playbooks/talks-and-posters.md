# Talks and posters

A talk or poster from your own work, someone else's paper or a study in
progress, including a re-cut of an existing deck. Details are in
[presentations](../../presentations/SKILL.md).

1. Name the audience, the slot and the one question, and say which kind of
   talk this is: own work, re-cut, paper, group-meeting update or poster.
2. Read the source material in full: the accepted report or `RESULT.md` and
   the plotbook, the paper, or the existing deck, and what the person who
   asked for the talk said they want.
3. Write the storyline with every field of the storyline contract; record
   your own choices in it as agent defaults.
4. Build the skeleton from the template: every main frame with its claim and
   a placeholder for its figure. Check it against the brief and get the
   presenter's confirmation on the rendered pages.
5. Polish: reuse the reviewed figures, generate every number from its owner,
   add no claim the written work does not support. Hand the presenter the
   source and keep their edits word for word.
6. On every build run the request asserts and the render gate with
   `--profile slides`, and fix blocking findings in the source
   ([render-review](../../render-review/SKILL.md)).
7. Review in separate tracks: claims at each change of source; frames at
   projection size by an independent reviewer at the three milestones
   (skeleton, the draft for the person named in the brief, final); the
   storyline and slides by a copy reviewer when others will judge the talk.
8. Write the rehearsal pack with the first full draft. After the presenter's
   timed rehearsal, bring its findings back into the deck.
9. Export the PowerPoint copy if the storyline lists it, deliver to the user's
   review inbox if the project has one, and say what awaits the presenter's
   review.
10. On the day of the talk apply late requests directly; after the upload
    write the closing record. Then mine the session for lessons
    ([reflect](../../reflect/SKILL.md)).

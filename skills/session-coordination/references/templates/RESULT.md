# Result

Outcome and scope of verification. Separate observation from inference.

## Artifacts and checks

Exact paths/revisions, numerical/source checks, review dispositions and gaps.

## Handoff

Recipient, safe continuation, delivery/notification/acceptance status separately.

## Leftovers

Owned changes, worktrees, jobs and files to preserve, with owner and retention
reason. Cleanup is a separate action; a result does not authorize deletion.
